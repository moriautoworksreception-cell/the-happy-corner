Our little story 💛

We believe children don't need much to have a wonderful time — just a little space, a little colour and the freedom to create.

So we created The Happy Corner: a playful corner where kids can forget about the screens for a while, use their imagination, discover colours and make something with their own hands.

And because parents already have enough to organise, we come to you in Dubai. You enjoy the celebration. We take care of the happy little corner.

Let them play. Let them create. Let them be little.

From painting activities and colouring sheets to candles and balloons for the celebration, we bring small details together to make a big, joyful memory.
